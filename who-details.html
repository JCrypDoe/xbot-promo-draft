Proof, Not Exposure

Most systems ask for full profile exposure. Earth3 flips that. PlayerPowerID can prove one fact at a time while your full history stays private.

What can be verified

  • Human + active participant status
  • Eligibility for a quest, reward, or sponsor offer
  • Threshold checks (stake, score, reputation band)
  • Whether a claim is valid, without leaking raw records

Why it matters

  • You keep control over your identity trail
  • Brands get trusted proof, not raw personal data
  • Governance stays auditable without becoming invasive
  • Player reputation compounds over time as reusable proof
Simple takeaway:

Build proof, not a giant public profile.
