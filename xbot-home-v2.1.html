HUD-WOW MUSIC ENGINE

Listen together. Promote together. Keep the reward tree bounded.

An open-source media player inside HUD-WoW can make synchronized party music optional. Music providers can choose open distribution or scarce Early Bird positions while each qualified engagement funds one bounded promotion pool.

Early Bird

Example: a provider may reserve a small number of high-percentage NodeRunner positions, limit attributable listeners and allow progressively broader downstream distribution.

One event, one pool

Do not duplicate full GXYZ rewards at every layer. The direct distributor, parent discovery node, listener and campaign pools split a defined event budget.

Rights stay separate

The media player can be open-source while the music carries its own rights for listening, streaming, VOD, clips or remixing.

Design rule: reward actual verified participation and attributable distribution, not unlimited recruitment ancestry.

JIX-ZEE FORMULAS

There should not be one universal PlayerPower score.

Different sponsors, musicians, games, players and future counterparties will care about different data points. The TimeChain holds ingredients. The requester chooses a recipe.

Human Juice

Real human, eligibility, region or age threshold.

Creator Juice

Creation history, provenance, remix compliance and disputes.

Node Juice

Audience quality, attributable discovery and bot resistance.

Music Juice

Discovery, genuine listening, promotion and artist support.

Collateral Juice

Threshold, duration, prior commitment and TimeChain context.

These are conceptual formulas, not fixed financial, identity or eligibility standards.

BACK THE ARTIST

GXYZ can show conviction, but money alone should not buy trust.

A player can choose to commit earned GXYZ back toward a musician or creator. The stronger idea is PlayerPower as a changing combination of commitment, trust, contribution and time.

PLAYERPOWER ˜

Commitment × Trust × Contribution × Time

The player who discovers, supports, promotes and remains useful builds a history that is harder to fake than a wallet balance.

Support without revealing everything

A future support program can ask for specific proofs rather than a whole identity or wallet history. The exact economics and legal rights would need to be defined by the actual implementation.

BUILD PROOF WITH GXYZ FORMULAS

Share the proof, not the whole kitchen.

A sponsor may care that a player is a real human in an eligible region. Another opportunity may care about Creator Juice or collateral thresholds. ZK-style proof design lets the request focus on the claim instead of exposing the complete TimeChain.
