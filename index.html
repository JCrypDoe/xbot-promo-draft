The moMintNTime Concept

When a real-world event can be verified by a digital event, something new becomes possible. Earth1 confirms what occurred, Earth2 records the digital action, and the result becomes a cryptographically verifiable moMintNTime.

These events are anchored through TimeChain references and Titanium Rails indexing so they remain queryable, permission-aware, and provable across the ecosystem.

Privacy Through Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Magnus ZK keeps activity protected behind mathematics. Players choose what to reveal, who can access it, and what value should be exchanged for that access.

Instead of platforms owning and monetizing player data, the player owns their digital history.

PlayerPower DigitalID

PlayerPowerID is the universal identity and reputation layer for all Earth3 users. It functions as a decentralized digital passport: one persistent ID across modules, games, markets, and governance systems.

Instead of fragmented logins, your identity follows you everywhere and grows with you. Unlike traditional username/password systems, PlayerPowerID is an on-chain identity token owned by the user, not a single platform.

Over time, it accumulates verifiable reputation signals through participation, play, governance voting, creation, and contribution.

Core Features

  • Unique On-Chain Identity Token: Cryptographically unique PlayerPowerID token minted at registration and user-controlled.
  • Reputation Score: Dynamic, time-weighted, non-transferable reputation from verified participation.
  • Linked Wallet + Profile: Wallet address, avatar, display name, and achievement badges in one portable record.
  • Cross-Module Interoperability: One identity recognized across all Earth3 modules.
  • Privacy Controls: Selective disclosure by default, with ZK proof routes for sensitive verification.
  • Achievement Badges: Verifiable credentials from milestones/events via the moMintNTime engine.

aDaB Game Token

The aDaB Advantage: most game tokens are optimized for platform operators first and players second. aDaB is framed to align long-term player value with ecosystem growth.

Fixed-supply alignment: aDaB is capped at 45 billion tokens, matching approximately 45 billion ADA. The economic narrative is 50% ADA (governance layer) and 50% BTC (store-of-value layer).

This pairing is designed to reduce the inflation-style dilution common in game economies and connect in-game effort, assets, and rewards to scarce digital-asset logic.

Why This Is Different

  • Scarcity-first design: fixed-supply framing instead of unlimited reward inflation.
  • Transparent monetary rules: known supply logic and mathematically verifiable ownership paths.

Titanium Rails · Index, Routing & Micro-Model Training Layer

Titanium Rails is the backbone index layer of Earth3: the circulatory system that keeps artifacts, payload pointers, stakes, and TimeChain references queryable, permission-aware, and cryptographically consistent.

It is Earth3’s directory + policy engine for deciding what exists, where it lives, who controls it, how it anchors to TimeChain, and what rules govern access.

Core Responsibilities

  • Global Artifact Index: canonical metadata for NFT identifiers, ownership, rarity/classification, NIGHT staked, DUST parameters, CPX² readiness, encrypted payload pointers, and TimeChain references.
  • Routing + Lookup: permission-correct query routing for sponsors, NodeRunners, and services (for example tier lookups, stake thresholds, or CPX²-ready payload discovery).
  • Access Policy Enforcement: policy blocks for player-controlled access, sponsor/node request gates, and training consent flags.
  • TimeChain Anchoring: links mint events, epoch activity, behavior updates, and sponsor interactions into cryptographically anchored timelines.
  • Payload Integrity Model: stores pointers + hashes (not raw history) to keep data modular, private, and verifiable.

Infinite LLM Micro-Model Training Data

Titanium Rails also serves as the substrate for Infinite LLM micro-model training: player-permissioned high-signal data atoms that can be filtered, packaged, and sold to many companies in many ways without exposing raw private history.

Each atom can be tagged with consent and training scope (`cpx2_ready`, `consent_flag`, `training_scope`, `sponsor_visibility`) so CPX² can train on legal, permissioned, TimeChain-anchored data streams.
