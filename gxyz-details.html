The Magic of Logic

WHERE

GXYZ is the coordination layer between identity, privacy, transactions, and sponsor interaction. It decides what signals move, what stays private, and what gets measured.

  • Selective disclosure: only the minimum necessary signal is revealed.
  • Proof without exposure: eligibility can be verified without exposing raw records.
  • Programmable trust: each route has policy, permissions, and payout logic.
