No More Forced Advertising

HOW

PayTooPlay flips the old model. Instead of being interrupted and tracked by default, the player sets terms first and only engages when the opportunity is worth it.

  • No forced ads: attention is opt-in, not extracted.
  • Player control: settings define what categories, offers, and reward bands are allowed.
  • Clear value exchange: participation is compensated, measurable, and permissioned.

Linked by Settings + GXYZ LoGiK

HOW

GXYZ LoGiK applies each player's privacy and routing rules in real time. Sponsors receive only the verified signal needed for a campaign event, not raw personal history.

Selective disclosureOnly minimum required signal is revealed for access, reward, or verification.
Proof without exposureEligibility can be confirmed without exposing the underlying private record.
Policy-based routingSignals are routed to the right module using player permissions and campaign rules.
Player ownershipThe player decides who can access data and under what compensation terms.

How Sponsors and Players Connect

HOW

PayTooPlay gives sponsors a clean, lawful path to participation while keeping the player in control.

  • Step 1: Sponsor publishes an opportunity with explicit criteria and budget logic.
  • Step 2: Player settings + GXYZ determine whether that opportunity can appear.
  • Step 3: Player chooses to engage or decline.
  • Step 4: Verified participation generates reward events that can anchor to TimeChain via moMintNTime.

Simple message: PayTooPlay means your attention is an asset you own, not a product someone else sells.

Why This Matters in Earth3

HOW
  • For Players: less noise, more control, direct value participation.
  • For Sponsors: cleaner intent signals and measurable engagement quality.
  • For Earth3: a sustainable model that aligns PlayerPower, privacy, and economic activity.
