The moMintNTime Concept

Proof of reality through cryptography and time

When a real-world event can be verified by a digital event, something new becomes possible. Earth1 confirms what occurred, Earth2 records the digital action, and the result is a cryptographically verifiable moMintNTime.

How It Works

1) Earth1 confirms

A real-world action happens: pickup, check-in, attendance, purchase, service completion, or verified creation.

2) Earth2 records

The digital event is logged with policy-aware identity signals, permissions, and event metadata.

3) TimeChain anchors

References are anchored so the record can be proven later without rewriting history.

4) Titanium Rails indexes

The event is queryable and permission-aware across PlayerPowerID, PayTooPlay, sponsors, and governance flows.

Remix + Royalty Example

An artist mints a song, a creator remixes it into a game variant, and later resale payouts route instantly to the right parties via smart-contract rules.
