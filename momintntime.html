The moMintNTime Concept

Proof of reality through cryptography and time

Earth1 confirms. Earth2 records. TimeChain anchors.

When a real-world event can be verified by a digital event, something new becomes possible. Earth1 confirms what occurred, Earth2 records the digital action, and the result is a cryptographically verifiable moMintNTime.

How It Works

1) Earth1 confirms

A real-world action happens: pickup, check-in, attendance, purchase, service completion, or verified creation.

2) Earth2 records

The digital event is logged with policy-aware identity signals, permissions, and event metadata.

3) TimeChain anchors

References are anchored so the record can be proven later without rewriting history.

4) Titanium Rails indexes

The event is queryable and permission-aware across PlayerPowerID, PayTooPlay, sponsors, and governance flows.

Remix + Royalty Example

One Moment, Many Looks

moMintNTime captures the truth once. moMintMode lets that same moment be rendered in different styles without changing the original record.

Skate moMinTemplate (Sample)

  • Player Doe uploads one Skate capture from a named spot.
  • Earth3 records who, where, when, and camera view as the source truth.
  • Doe picks REAL.v01 to morph the game frame into a realistic photo look.
  • The renderer upgrades materials, lighting, and texture realism while preserving pose, framing, and location geometry.
  • Output stays linked to the same moMintNTime, so proof and provenance remain clear.

Renderer Strategy

Yes, we can outsource image generation to providers like Grok. Earth3 keeps the moment record and rendering rules. Providers compete on output quality while the source proof stays portable and player-owned.
