What TimeChain Does

THEN

TimeChain is the proof timeline. It anchors key moments so they can be verified later without guesswork.

  • Event continuity: mints, interactions, and transitions keep a traceable chronology.
  • Cryptographic integrity: key records can be validated against hash references.
  • Shared confidence: players, sponsors, and nodes can verify the same event history.

Titanium Rails: The Index Spine

THEN

Titanium Rails is the metadata and policy backbone that maps artifacts, payload pointers, identity links, and TimeChain references into one coherent structure.

Global indexKnows what exists, who controls it, and how each artifact is classified.
Routing layerAnswers ecosystem queries with consistent, permission-correct access paths.
Policy engineEnforces consent and visibility rules across players, sponsors, and automation.
Proof linksConnects each artifact and payload to its TimeChain references.

Infinite Micro-Model Economy

THEN

Rails also powers Earth3's micro-model economy by turning consented, high-signal activity into trainable data atoms.

  • Player-owned atoms: data remains permissioned under PlayerPower and DigitalID controls.
  • Infinite packaging: the same source atoms can be sorted into many sponsor-ready datasets.
  • CPX2-ready streams: clean training feeds are delivered without exposing raw private history.

Simple message: TimeChain proves what happened. Titanium Rails makes that proof usable for value, trust, governance, and training at ecosystem scale.
